# Proposal

## Why

Members need a way to see consistency across their work on Arcshift.

## What Changes

- Show a 365-day heat map, active days, and streaks on the member dashboard.
- Derive activity from existing authenticated records and group it by the member's local calendar.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `career-activity` covers member activity counts and the heat map.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Member dashboard, private activity endpoint, and lookup indexes.
